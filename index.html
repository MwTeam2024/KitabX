How credits work
📖 List a book

You receive a pending credit as soon as you list a book.

✅ Give it away

Once the handover is verified, your pending credit becomes available to spend.

🤝 Request a book

Requesting another book reserves 1 credit from your available balance.

📥 Receive a book

Once that handover is verified, the reserved credit is permanently deducted.

↩️ Rejected, cancelled or expired

Any reserved credit tied to that request is released back to your available balance automatically.
